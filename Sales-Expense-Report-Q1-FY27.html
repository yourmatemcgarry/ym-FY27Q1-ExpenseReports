Sales Department Expense Report – Q1 FY27

Your Mates Brewing Co. · Jul–Sep 2026 · Prepared 1 Nov 2026 for Christen McGarry · All figures ex-GST from Xero

The Sales department spent $143.3k in Q1 FY27: $52.0k (27%) under the $195.3k budget and 4% above Q1 FY26 ($138.2k). The total includes a $28.9k Sunshine Coast Lightning invoice for FY26 games, booked on 15 Jul. Without it, underlying spend was $114.4k, 17% below last year.

Most urgent: event revenue is being booked as negative expense, the 28 Sep pay run looks like it paid leave on top of a full week, and two possible duplicates need checking (Amarok service, Nexburg coolers). Overlapping software subscriptions are the clearest saving.

Account summary

Each Sales account vs last quarter, the same quarter last year and the Your Mates Xero File Budget, sorted by Q1 spend.

Q4 FY26 is understated by $45.0k of credits from Your Mates Bowls Pub for Sunshine Coast Lightning bar operations, booked against Events; excluding them, Q4 spend was $119.9k, so Q1 is up 20% on Q4. The budget looks set on a different basis in places: Freight ($34.0k), Trade Spend Beer ($22.7k) and Digital Ads ($20.0k) sit well above any recent quarter; on-costs are budgeted at ~27% of wages but run at 12% (super only); Bonus, Annual Leave and Design & Content have no budget.

Incorrect or miscoded transactions

Twelve items to fix or check, most important first. The Lightning cut-off ($28.9k) alone moves the quarter by 20%.

Smaller recodes: Stradbroke ferry $278.99 (Parking & Tolls → Travel); AutoPod plugin $45.98 (Design & Content → Subscriptions); Hospitality Superstore cold cups $199 (Traditional → Events). Accruals likely missing: no September BP fuel card statement (August was $1,980), no September account-tab journal for Brand Spend Beer or Brewpub Hospitality, and no Swigg Power BI / Microsoft 365 charge ($158.60/month) for Aug or Sep.

Price increases over time

Like-for-like line items, Jul 2025 – Sep 2026. Freight still fell 13% on last year because fewer drops went to ATT; the rate rise is hidden by volume mix, not a better price.

Spend to cut or that looks overpriced

Overlapping software is the easiest saving: about $4k–$9k a year depending on which tools go. The Swigg BrandPrint termination in August already removes about $19k a year.
