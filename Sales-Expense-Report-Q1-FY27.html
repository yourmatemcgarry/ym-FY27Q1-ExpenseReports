Sales Department Expense Report – Q1 FY27

Your Mates Brewing Co. · Jul–Sep 2026 · Updated 15 Nov 2026 for Christen McGarry · All figures ex-GST from Xero

The Sales department spent $115.1k in Q1 FY27: $80.3k (41%) under the $195.3k budget and 17% below Q1 FY26 ($138.2k). Since the last report the $28.9k Sunshine Coast Lightning invoice for FY26 games has been re-dated to 30 Jun 2026, so it no longer sits in Q1, and the September account-tab journal, a $500 Criteo (Endeavour) ad and two Amaysim bills have been posted.

Most urgent now: event revenue is still being booked as negative expense, the 28 Sep pay run looks like it paid leave on top of a full week, and two possible duplicates need checking (Amarok service, Nexburg coolers). Overlapping software subscriptions are the clearest saving.
