Brew Pub Expense Report – Q1 FY27

Your Mates Brewing Co. · Jul–Sep 2026 · Prepared 2 Nov 2026 for Christen McGarry · All figures ex-GST from Xero

Brew Pub operating expenses were $133.0k in Q1 FY27, $6.5k (5%) over the $126.5k budget and 9% up on Q1 FY26 ($122.5k). This report covers expenses only; direct costs (food, drinks, merchandise and bar and kitchen wages, which Xero classes as Cost of Sales) are left out.

Rent is the main driver, up $13.1k on last year (a new RWC rate since Q2 FY26 plus an extra $5.2k invoice in September). Music, hospitality equipment and print are also well over budget. Management wages are $16.5k lower because the manager's pay is now coded to Shared Expenses.
