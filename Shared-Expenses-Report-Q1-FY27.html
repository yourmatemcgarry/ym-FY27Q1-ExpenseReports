Shared Expenses Report – Q1 FY27

Your Mates Brewing Co. · Jul–Sep 2026 · Prepared 2 Nov 2026 for Christen McGarry · All figures ex-GST from Xero

Shared Expenses show $210.7k for Q1 FY27, $22.9k under budget and 38% below last year. Most of that gap is timing, not savings. No depreciation is booked (last year $93.9k a quarter), and a $27.6k payroll-tax refund, likely for FY26, turns Q1 payroll tax into a credit. No business insurance premium is booked either (budget $21.2k).

The real cost movement is interest, at $42.3k vs $4.7k last year, including a single $22.9k CBA trade-loan interest journal. Admin and management wages are steady. 98 transactions ($7.6k) have no receipt.
