Operations Expense Report – Q1 FY27

Your Mates Brewing Co. · Jul–Sep 2026 · Prepared 2 Nov 2026 for Christen McGarry · All figures ex-GST from Xero

Operations shows $614.0k for Q1 FY27, half of last year's $1,187.7k and $567.8k under budget. That isn't a real saving: Q1 cost of goods hasn't been fully booked. There's no brewing-materials cost at all (Q1 FY26 $292.5k), no September excise journal (July $156.1k, August $77.4k), and almost no Container Refund Scheme cost ($627 vs $51.5k).

Until those journals are posted, Q1 gross profit is overstated by roughly $400k+ and the P&L shouldn't be shared. Underlying running costs look steady: rent is up 6–7%, gas is down 20% and electricity is down 15% on last year.
