Bowls Pub / Crackerjack Expense Report – Q1 FY27

Your Mate Bowlsy Pty Ltd (Bowls Pub) · Jul–Sep 2026 · Updated 15 Nov 2026 for Christen McGarry · All figures ex-GST from Xero

The Bowls Pub (Crackerjack) spent $142.3k on operating expenses in Q1 FY27: $31.2k (28%) over the $111.2k budget and 19% up on Q1 FY26 ($119.9k). Cost of sales (food, drinks and bar and kitchen wages) is left out, as in the Brew Pub report.

Rent is most of the overspend. Since the last report Good Mates Property Trust 2 has added $1,000 a month of extra FY27 outgoings and a $1,437.59 a month FY26 outgoings adjustment, taking Q1 rent to $60.6k against a $37.0k budget. Digital ads, music and equipment are also over budget. Insurance and depreciation look under budget only because they haven't been booked.
